HOW IT WORKS

The GPA formula

Multiply each course's grade point by its credit units. Add those results, then divide by the total credit units.

GPA = Σ (grade point × credit units) ÷ Σ credit units

Example: A (5 points) in a 3-unit course and B (4 points) in a 2-unit course gives (15 + 8) ÷ 5 = 4.60.
